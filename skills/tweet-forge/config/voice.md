# Voice profile (template)

Describe how your posts sound. The skill writes in this voice from the
first word. Replace every section with your own rules.

## Register
One paragraph on the register: e.g. forensic analyst, dry humor,
institutional third person, plain diction.

## Patterns (use these)
List 4-8 labeled moves you actually use, e.g.:
- number-first hook: open with the figure, then the mechanism
- falsification dare: state what would prove the claim wrong
- contrast pair: put two figures side by side and let the gap do the work
- arithmetic demolition: walk the math, step by step, to a verdict
- named-mechanism closer: end by naming the process that produced the number

## Anti-patterns (never use these)
List what you never do, e.g.: em dashes, semicolons, hashtags,
engagement bait ("thoughts?"), questions left hanging.

## Calibration samples
Paste 3-5 of your own past posts here. The skill reads these before
drafting, so the voice stays yours instead of a description of yours.
