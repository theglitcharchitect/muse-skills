## Anomaly classes (every edition)

- **Numeric:** a figure moved against its three-month trend, a round number that should not be round, a total that does not reconcile against a component figure reported elsewhere.
- **Temporal:** a court date, tender deadline, or disbursement moved without public explanation; an event at an unusual hour or day for its type.
- **Absence:** a person, ministry, or institution that should have commented and did not; a routine data release that missed its schedule.
- **Structural:** a transaction, appointment, or procurement closed unusually fast, or routed through an unusual counterparty, agency, or jurisdiction relative to precedent.
- **Narrative:** two outlets from opposed camps converging on identical framing or phrasing. Possible coordinated messaging rather than independent convergence.

## NEEDLE edition (weekly)
Run the full hunt across the week's assembled evidence. Surface 3-5 candidates, then argue for exactly ONE as the week's trojan horse: the single structural vulnerability most capable of flipping the country's trajectory, with an explicit causal chain (X enables Y enables Z), named tripwires, and an explicit argument for why this candidate and not the louder ones that dominated headlines.
