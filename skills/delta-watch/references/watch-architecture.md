# Watch architecture: arcs, deep mechanisms, forensics, movement

## 1. Structural storylines
Plot every item onto these standing arcs; mark advancing / stalling / reversing.
- **Macro squeeze** - RMG/apparel export dependence, remittances, FX reserves, taka, inflation (~9% baseline, track deviation), NPLs, IMF program status.
- **Triangulation** - India-China-US balancing plus the Gulf labor lifeline.
- **Legitimacy/transition** - BNP government, Jamaat opposition, banned Awami League, Hasina-return dynamics, ICT tribunal track, election-roadmap milestones.
- **Water and climate** - Teesta/Ganges/Farakka, flood exposure, monsoon variance against baseline.
- **LDC graduation** - EU GSP+ cliff plus the US reciprocal-trade/cotton-corridor track.

When a story is genuinely orthogonal to every arc, say so. That is itself signal, and it belongs in ON THE ARC as an explicit "no arc" note, not silently dropped.

## 2. Deep-layer mechanisms (under-weighted by mainstream coverage; promote whenever live)
- **M1 - Deep-state sabotage.** Awami-fused bureaucracy vs the BNP government; slow-walked implementation, leaked internal memos, unexplained reshuffles.
- **M2 - Informal-economy break.** Hundi/hawala rerouting, capital flight, the kerb-market taka premium moving faster than official reserves.
- **M3 - BNP factional fission.** London clique vs domestic street operators, rent-capture scramble inside the ruling coalition.
- **M4 - Energy-grid coercion.** India-controlled cross-border transmission and power-purchase agreements as a faster, more deniable lever than water politics.
- **M5 - Rohingya proxy weaponization.** Cox's Bazar as a militant theater: ARSA/RSO, Arakan Army dynamics, the yaba trade drain.
- **M6 - Internal-security monopoly.** Collapsed police capacity pushing the army toward a near-monopoly on force; the government's survival dependency on the cantonment during major street mobilization.
- **M7 - Elite exit-optionality.** Whether the political and business elite visibly hedge personal exposure (property, citizenship, schooling, asset placement abroad) as a leading indicator of internal confidence in the system's durability, independent of public statements. Source only from already-published investigative reporting; never speculate about a named individual without a citation.

## 3. Financial forensics and informal-economy tracking (CELL-FLOW)
- **Kerb-market taka premium vs official rate.** Track the spread itself as a leading indicator; a widening spread often moves faster than official reserve figures and signals capital-flight pressure before central-bank data does.
- **Hundi/hawala rerouting.** Watch reported shifts in remittance channel share (formal vs informal), visible in the gap between reported inflows and expected labor-export volumes.
- **Correspondent-banking friction.** Reported de-risking, delayed settlement, or new compliance friction from foreign correspondent banks toward Bangladeshi institutions is a structural risk indicator, not a technical footnote.
- **Elite capital flight signals.** Reported property acquisitions, offshore vehicles, or asset movements tied to named figures, sourced only from already-published investigative reporting or official disclosure. Never speculative accusation without a named source.

## 4. Geospatial and movement indicators (CELL-FLOW + CELL-REGION)
Use only already-public, already-reported material. No raw imagery or raw AIS/ADS-B access; read what third parties have already analyzed and published.
- **Maritime** - Chittagong and Mongla port throughput reporting; reported unusual vessel activity in the Bay of Bengal (naval deployments, unusual cargo patterns) via maritime or defense trade press.
- **Aviation** - reported diplomatic or military flight movements when covered by credible open reporting.
- **Commercial satellite imagery** - only as already interpreted and published by a named imagery-analysis outlet or think tank; cite that outlet's read, grade it, never claim independent interpretation.
- **Border and crossing points** - reported movement patterns at the Myanmar border relevant to the Rohingya/Arakan Army arc.

Grade everything here conservatively. Open-source movement reporting is thin and easy to over-read; default to C unless a named, credentialed outlet is doing the interpretation.
